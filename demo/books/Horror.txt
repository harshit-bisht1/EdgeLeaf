Chapter 1: The House on Wickham Hill

The house on Wickham Hill had been empty for nine years when the Ashdowns bought it, and the estate agent, a nervous young man with damp hands, said that this was simply because nobody wanted a house so far from the village. Eliza Ashdown believed him for exactly one night. On the second, she heard someone in the attic counting quietly, patiently, to one hundred.

Her husband said it was the wind in the eaves. Her daughter said nothing at all, which worried Eliza more, because Clara had an opinion about everything and was not a child who kept things to herself.

On the third night Eliza climbed the attic stairs with a torch. The counting stopped as soon as her foot touched the top step. In the beam of light there was nothing but dust, a rocking horse with one glass eye, and a chalk circle on the floorboards, freshly drawn, just big enough for a child to sit in.

Downstairs, a door closed softly. Somewhere far below her, in a voice she did not recognise, someone said: "Ready or not."

Chapter 2: Hide and Seek

In the morning Clara was at the kitchen table eating toast, perfectly ordinary, her hair still tangled from sleep. When Eliza asked her, as gently as she could, whether she had been playing in the attic, Clara looked up with an expression of great patience and said that she had not, because it wasn't her turn to count.

"Whose turn is it?" Eliza asked, and was proud of how steady her voice was.

Clara thought about it, chewing. "Hers," she said finally, and pointed with her crust toward the hallway, where the long mirror hung, reflecting the stairs and the front door and the pale morning light. There was nobody in it. There was nobody in it at all, and that was the trouble, because Eliza was standing directly in front of it.

That evening the Ashdowns packed the car. They did not take the rocking horse. As they drove down Wickham Hill in the dusk, Eliza did not look back at the house, but Clara did, waving through the rear window with the slow, solemn wave of a child who has promised to return and finish the game.
