Chapter 5: Above the Clouds

Above the clouds the air was so thin that breathing became a kind of prayer, and Li Wen climbed for nine days with Elder Mei's staff in his hand and her last instructions in his ears. The peak, when he reached it, was nothing but bare rock and wind, and in the middle of it stood a second plum tree, its branches black against the stars.

It was smaller than the one in the courtyard, and older. When he laid his hand on its bark, it spoke to him in the voice of a girl.

Chapter 6: The Plum Tree Remembers

"You took your time," the tree said. "I planted your tree for you three hundred years ago, so that you would know the way when you came. I was beginning to think you never would." A single blossom opened on the topmost branch, pale as the moon, and the wind fell quiet all across the mountain.

Li Wen bowed until his forehead touched the stone. Far below, in the courtyard of the Azure Cloud Sect, every plum tree on the mountain burst into flower at once, and the outer disciples woke in the dark to the scent of spring and did not know why they were weeping.
