Chapter 3: The Trial of the Outer Disciples

Once a year the Azure Cloud Sect held its trial for the outer disciples, and once a year the sweeping boy was not invited. This year the elders sent for Li Wen by name, which caused a great deal of whispering, and placed him in the first round against Zhou Kang, who had broken three opponents' arms the year before and liked to tell people about it.

Zhou Kang struck first, as he always did, with a palm that cracked the stone of the arena. Li Wen stepped aside, the way a branch moves in the wind, and the palm passed through the place where he had been. He did not strike back. He simply stood there, breathing, while the scent of plum blossom filled the arena in the middle of summer.

Chapter 4: An Elder Takes Notice

Elder Mei had not left her pavilion in eleven years. She came down the mountain on the evening after the trial, leaning on a staff of white wood, and the disciples scattered before her like sparrows. She found Li Wen beneath the plum tree, as everyone knew she would, and sat down beside him on the frozen ground without a word.

"That tree," she said at last, "has not flowered in three hundred years. Not since the day my master planted it. Tell me, boy. What did you say to it?"
