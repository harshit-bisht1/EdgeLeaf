Chapter 1: The Sect at the Foot of the Mountain

Li Wen was sixteen when he climbed the nine thousand steps of the Azure Cloud Sect with nothing but a straw hat, a cracked jade token and the stubborn conviction that his spiritual roots were not, as three separate elders had told him, the worst they had ever seen. He reached the top at dusk, soaked in sweat, and was promptly given a broom and told to sweep the courtyard.

The outer disciples laughed at him for a month. He swept the courtyard every morning before they woke and every evening after they slept, and in between he sat beneath the old plum tree at the edge of the cliff and breathed, the way the manuals said, drawing the thin mountain qi into his body one patient thread at a time.

Nothing happened. Then, on the thirty-first night, something did. A single plum blossom drifted down from the bare tree, in the middle of winter, and settled on the back of his hand. It was warm.

Chapter 2: The Plum Blossom Method

The blossom did not wither. Li Wen kept it in the fold of his robe, and each night it grew a little warmer, until one evening it dissolved into light and sank into his skin, and he understood, with a clarity that left him gasping, that he had been breathing the wrong way all his life.

He breathed the right way until dawn. When he opened his eyes, the courtyard was silent, the outer disciples were standing in their doorways in their sleeping robes, and the broom in his hand had turned to jade.
